# Team 4 — Claim-by-claim primary-source audit

**Reviewed:** November 4, 2026 | **Input:** unchanged [LAB matrix](04_Laboratory_Evidence_Matrix.md), v0.9 (November 3) | **Method:** targeted verification of publicly accessible primary guidelines, trial abstracts and standards against the exact wording of all 42 records; no systematic search, no external clinical signoff.

This audit **covers every LAB-001–LAB-042 record individually**, but is **not** a full paragraph-by-paragraph appraisal of every cited publication, trial bias assessment, or clinical release approval. A status of `SUPPORTED_SCOPE` means the **narrow general claim** was corroborated in an inspected primary source or authoritative abstract—not that it predicts individual patient benefit, validates a treatment decision, or passes the review gates. All source references below are research citations, not embedded clinical decision rules.

## Important corrections and boundary findings

1. **2026 lipid guideline corrections — individually checked:** [June 22, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001457) changed the ApoB-related triglyceride example in Take-Home Message 5 from **>200 mg/dL to ≥150 mg/dL**, corrected two figure references, and corrected a reviewer name. [September 8, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001475) corrected four bile-acid-sequestrant table entries, notably a **colesevelam unit error (3.75 mg → 3.75 g)** and administration frequencies. **None is authority to prescribe**; the app must not publish medication doses. These corrections do not alter the narrow generic diet statements in LAB-008/011/012, but the amended version (not outdated summaries) must be cited if ApoB details are added. [Original guideline](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001423); [June publisher notice](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/pdf/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001457?download=true); [September institutional author-archive abstract](https://pure.johnshopkins.edu/en/publications/correction-to-2026-accahaaacvprabcacpmadaagsaphaaspcnlapcna-guide/).
2. **An unsupported citation is not equivalent to a false clinical hypothesis.** LAB-029 cites an ADA nutrition source for thyroid testing; LAB-030 cites a CKD guideline for asymptomatic gout; LAB-019 and LAB-027 have inadequate specificity. These need replacement disease-appropriate references. No thyroid, gout, mineral or folate algorithm is approved.
3. **Population is not a laboratory correction factor:** NGSP confirms HbA1c interference is **assay and variant-specific**; KDIGO 2024 explicitly discourages race-based eGFR. Do not infer genotype or substitute ancestry for a measurement.
4. **Different outcome layers:** the DPP's 58% reduction reflects a structured intervention in a trial population; the Portfolio diet's −0.73 mmol/L LDL change is a lipid outcome (not an individual ideal-weight change or direct event benefit for that particular pattern).
5. **Clinical severity is not an app verdict:** source-level lab criticality differs by specimen, method, laboratory and symptoms; independent thresholds and individualized dietary restrictions remain out of scope.
