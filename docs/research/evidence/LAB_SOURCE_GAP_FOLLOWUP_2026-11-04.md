# Follow-up source review: seven laboratory citation gaps

**Date:** November 4, 2026  
**Scope:** targeted check of replacement clinical guidelines/consensus statements for LAB-007, LAB-019, LAB-022, LAB-027, LAB-029, LAB-030 and LAB-037.  
**Decision:** `CANDIDATE_REFERENCE_FOUND` does **not** mean `SUPPORTED_SCOPE`. All seven original `SOURCE_GAP` dispositions remain unchanged until a claim-by-claim, source-version-specific appraisal closes the exact mismatch. **No new diagnostic or nutritional rule is authorized.**

Read with the [unchanged original Team 4 evidence matrix](04_Laboratory_Evidence_Matrix.md) and [original 42-claim audit](04_LAB_CLAIM_AUDIT_2026-11-04.md). The latter remains the controlling disposition register for this phase.

**LAB-040:** This review does **not** change its `WITHHELD` status. A source on thyroid, insulin, calcium, folate, gout or lipids is not a derivation or validation of an individual lab-adjusted ideal-weight equation.

## Priority and interpretation

1. **Citations improved, algorithms still withheld.** For LAB-007, -022, -029, -030 and -037, a relevant source now replaces an *off-topic original source as a candidate*, but a source about a diagnostic process does not justify a diagnosis or personalized nutrition calculation.
2. **Limited population scope.** LAB-019 is a CKD-specific monitoring guideline; LAB-027 combines overlapping B12, folate and iron questions with sources that differ in scope and currency. Do not transfer one subpopulation's biochemical monitoring recommendations to everyone.
3. **Medical context missing in the form.** Pregnancy, medications, symptom severity, assay interference, specimen type, diagnosed disease, chronicity and medical history are not systematically captured. Do not use absent information to clear a person for diet changes.
4. **No reclassification in the 136-claim index.** These seven entries are intentionally left as `SOURCE_GAP` until a narrower exact claim can be written and independently reviewed. The 94 earlier ancestry, nutrition and pharmacogenetic entries are still explicitly not audited in this pass.
5. **No physician-facing replacement advice yet.** Source links above serve provenance and future expert review; this addendum itself must not be used as an application clinical decision table.

## Release gate for a future evidence status change

A reviewer must quote or paraphrase a precisely bounded claim; confirm publication/version, population, measure, assay and limits; assess whether a suitable primary standard actually supports **each clause**; document contradiction and clinical-transportability risks; record an explicit rationale and any remaining uncertainty; update the original audit and 136-claim register together; and independently test any proposed public wording. **Do not automatically upgrade all seven statuses because a relevant journal guideline was located.**

This follow-up was performed by targeted review of available guideline text, not a systematic literature review, independent clinical signoff, or validation of any patient-specific intervention.
