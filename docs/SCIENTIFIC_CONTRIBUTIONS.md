# Scientific collaboration and evidence-submission policy

Ancestry Health welcomes constructive contributions from clinicians, registered dietitians, biomedical researchers, pharmacists, geneticists, epidemiologists, statisticians, public-health specialists, software developers, students, and people with lived experience. **You do not need credentials or an institutional affiliation to raise an evidence concern or contribute.** Experience can identify important research questions; scientific statements must still be evaluated on their evidence.

This is a free, open-source **educational** project. It is not a diagnostic, prescribing, or clinically validated medical device. Suggestions are discussion proposals, not clinical recommendations.

## How to participate

1. **Share a source or original research:** [Submit scientific evidence](https://github.com/PortalHunter/Ethnicy-Based-Optimal-Weight/issues/new?template=01-scientific-evidence.md). Published papers, guidelines, replications, negative results and well-labeled preprints are welcome.
2. **Challenge a claim:** [Question or correct scientific evidence](https://github.com/PortalHunter/Ethnicy-Based-Optimal-Weight/issues/new?template=02-evidence-correction.md). Cite the exact statement or method when possible. Corrections, contradictions and retractions are highly valued.
3. **Suggest a new module:** [Propose an additional module](https://github.com/PortalHunter/Ethnicy-Based-Optimal-Weight/issues/new?template=03-module-proposal.md). New scope is welcome if connected to the mission and feasible to explain safely.
4. **Improve accessibility or usability:** [Share feedback](https://github.com/PortalHunter/Ethnicy-Based-Optimal-Weight/issues/new?template=04-general-feedback.md).
5. **Contribute text, tests or code:** Open a [pull request](https://github.com/PortalHunter/Ethnicy-Based-Optimal-Weight/pulls) following [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md), or first open an issue to discuss a larger change. GitHub issues and pull requests support public threaded comments.

All submissions are public. Never include identifiable patient histories, medical records, raw DNA files or other private medical information. Use published aggregate findings or synthetic test cases. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md), not a public issue.

## Evidence review: what we look for

- **Traceability:** exact citation, DOI/PMID or stable link, version, publication status, corrections/retractions, and whether a source is primary research, systematic review, guideline, preprint or commentary.
- **Population and endpoint:** who was studied, design, comparison, setting/jurisdiction, sample size, measured outcome, time horizon and relevance to the proposed Ancestry Health wording.
- **Uncertainty and counterevidence:** effect estimates and uncertainty where available, confounders, bias, generalizability, harms, disagreement and evidence gaps. A result in one population is not a personal prediction.
- **Conceptual precision:** distinguish race, self-described ancestry/heritage, geography, genetic variants, family history and diagnosed disease. Do not impute an individual's genotype, disease status, medication response or ideal weight from population identity.
- **Practical consequences:** identify whether the proposed change is a citation, explanatory revision, hypothesis, optional educational feature, or change to a health-related calculation. The scrutiny required increases with potential harm.
- **Transparency:** disclose relevant funding, authorship, professional interests or product affiliations when material. Optional credentials help assign review expertise but neither confer automatic authority nor determine whether criticism is welcome.

A source can be sound without supporting the proposed implementation. We may record an item as **corroborated in a narrow context, qualified, needing additional sources, contradicted, or withheld**. A paper is not a license to prescribe treatment, imply a personal benefit, or adjust the calculator's weight estimate.

For changes to clinical claims, ancestry groupings, BMI thresholds, laboratory interpretation, nutrition suggestions or pharmacogenetics, follow the existing [evidence integration rules](EVIDENCE_INTEGRATION.md) and [clinical review checklist](process/CLINICAL_REVIEW.md). Domain-qualified independent appraisal and maintainer approval are needed for higher-impact claims. Automated checks do not substitute for human scientific review.

## Module proposals and scope

Potential modules can include educational reviews of nutritional patterns, family-history interpretation, lab concepts, validated pharmacogenetic literature, body composition, or additional population research. A module proposal should specify its educational question, sources, boundaries, optional inputs, accessibility and privacy implications, safety exclusions, and how it relates back to the project's purpose.

**Research first, software second.** Not every supported study requires a calculator. Prefer clear reading material and clinician discussion questions when personalized interpretation would require unavailable clinical context. Diagnostic classifications, medication choice/dosing, genotype inference, automatic lab-derived ideal-weight targets and individualized treatment plans are outside the current release scope.

## Review outcomes and credit

Maintainers may discuss a submission openly, request clarification or a counter-source, add it to an evidence review, ask for subject-matter expertise, defer implementation, or decline it with a documented rationale. **Submitting research does not guarantee publication or merge.** Open corrections are welcome even when they call current methods into question. Review does not amount to clinical validation.

Contributors are credited through GitHub authorship and contribution history where applicable. Citation credit remains with original study authors. If you want recognition in a document, please specify the attribution you prefer; do not supply private contact details in public threads.

## Licensing and third-party materials

The software is [MIT-licensed](../LICENSE), and eligible original educational materials may be offered under the project's [CC BY 4.0 content notice](../LICENSE-CONTENT.md), subject to its exact scope. By opening an issue, you are **not** automatically relicensing the full text of a paper you cite or granting us publication rights to material owned by others. Contributors who propose original prose, code, figures or datasets for inclusion must confirm appropriate rights and permissions and agree to the applicable contribution license before merge.

Link to copyrighted research rather than copying full texts, restricted figures or proprietary datasets. Disclose preprints and unpublished findings as such and avoid treating unreviewed work or public figures' claims as established medical evidence.

## Community conduct

Address arguments and methods, not people's identity or credentials. Welcome minority and contradictory findings when backed by an assessable source. Be respectful toward participants and populations discussed; avoid stereotypes or unsupported group generalizations. Do not use this repository to market personal services, supplements, diet programs, or medical products. Personal medical emergencies and individualized treatment questions belong with qualified care providers, not public issue threads.
