## C. Relationship edges

Use `unknown` by default. For any relationship hypothesis, specify A ID, B ID, label (independent_telling, same_work_edition, translation, copy, adaptation, cross_cultural_analogue, disputed), evidence URL, reviewer and date. **Cross-cultural analogy does not prove descent from a shared tale**.

## E. Negative controls
