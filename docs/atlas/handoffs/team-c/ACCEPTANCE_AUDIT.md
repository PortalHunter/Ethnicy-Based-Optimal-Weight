## Scope resolution

The original task calls for sampled article candidates and explicit geographic coverage gaps. It does not require Team C to clinically appraise every paper or turn 1,736 queue items into a registered systematic review. That wider work is unfinished and must not be implied complete. “Complete” here always means **discovery handoff with documented source/coverage limitations**. There is no permission to publish personalized medical claims, genetic predictions or changes to calculations.

Unresolved primary-source barriers are maintained in GAPS.md, not erased to manufacture a perfect directory. A candidate-only lead is not counted among verified journal identity claims.
