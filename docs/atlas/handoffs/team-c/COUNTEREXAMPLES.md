# Research visibility counterexamples (evidence-driven discovery tests, 2026-11-04)

These are examples of **records that an English-title-only / high-impact-general-journal-only strategy can overlook**. This does **not** assert that any paper is absent from PubMed or lacks an English abstract; English searching may still retrieve some. These are *discoverability* cases, not scientific endorsements. Confirmed exact publication/archival links are provided.

1. **Japanese original title / diabetes** — [2026 journal archive](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/browse/tonyobyo/list/-char/ja) and [Japanese diabetes paper](https://doi.org/10.11213/tonyobyo.69.450). Search `糖尿病`, not only diabetes in English. J-STAGE journal identity verified.
2. **Indonesian gout** — [2014 original-language study](https://journal.ugm.ac.id/jgki/article/view/18879) `Pola makan, obesitas, dan frekuensi serangan pada pasien artritis gout`; Indonesian phrase/title is useful before filtering to English-only publications.
3. **Portuguese Brazilian lactose / labeling** — [2018 original study](https://www.scielo.br/j/csc/a/4Qh3Rd46QRJky4nnvqWHrPk/). It is an audit of package labeling (not a clinical lactase study), discovered with local terminology.
4. **Portuguese Brazilian hemochromatosis** — [historic review](https://www.scielo.br/j/rbhh/a/YGX8c3fghWkWCDntKCgtxMN/) uses the original phrase `hemocromatose hereditária`; historic narrative review not current genetic risk evidence.
5. **Portuguese dyslipidemia guidance** — [2025 Sociedade Brasileira de Cardiologia guideline](https://www.scielo.br/j/abc/a/tRJrwGzKX6C4GvMqdJpZcGk/); Brazilian Portuguese scope and country-specific guideline are separate from indexed English trials.
6. **Spanish Chilean celiac** — [2016 original-language review](https://www.scielo.cl/scielo.php?pid=S0034-98872016000200010&script=sci_arttext); a dated overview, not a clinical guideline.
7. **Spanish Spanish-hospital celiac** — [2013 Spain case-control study](https://scielo.isciii.es/scielo.php?pid=S1130-01082013000900005&script=sci_arttext&tlng=es); hospital setting not ancestry.
8. **Vietnamese Tiền Giang hypertension** — [2025 original-language school study](https://tapchiyhocvietnam.vn/index.php/vmj/article/view/16738); local setting and adolescent sample are discoverable through `tăng huyết áp`.
9. **Vietnamese Ba Vì hypertension** — [2026 hospital study](https://tapchiyhocvietnam.vn/index.php/vmj/article/view/17436), with original local facility identification and cross-sectional design.
10. **Thai lipids/type-2 diabetes** — [2026 cohort paper](https://he02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/TJHP/article/view/279103); source has original Thai title and retrospective cohort description; dyslipidemia is NOT synonymous with elevated LDL.
11. **Persian diabetes and metabolism periodical** — [2026 Persian-language journal archive](https://www.magiran.com/magazine/2991) with [national ISSN record](https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/2345-4008); identity established, article-level quality and journal peer review still pending.
12. **French-language Mali periodical** — [Mali Médical original-language archive](https://www.malimedical.org/) is locally discoverable even if no paper has yet been selected for clinical appraisal; a *journal-level* counterexample, not an article-level clinical finding.

**Method caution:** These are 12 demonstrations of why *restrictive discovery rules* are unsound. They are not 12 proven PubMed omissions; such absence requires independent index-specific record searches.
