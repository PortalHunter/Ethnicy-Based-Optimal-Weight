## Legal ledger state definitions and upgrade gates

`identity_baseline` is read directly from `countries.csv`: `PROVISIONAL_IDENTITY` or `UNKNOWN_REVIEW_NEEDED`; it does not establish a sole or exclusive authority.

`legal_review_stage`: `UNSCORED_EXISTING_RESEARCH` (initial), `IN_PROGRESS`, `PARTIAL_SOURCE_CHECKED`, `RESEARCH_COMPLETE_PENDING_EXPERT_REVIEW`. Initial `UNSCORED` is a **workflow flag**, not negative evidence.

Each of eight legal dimensions (`establishing_law`, `current_amendments`, `policy_public_health`, `medicines_professional_regulation`, `surveillance_institutes`, `devolution_territorial_scope`, `independent_issuer_checks`, `effective_dates_conflicts`) may be:
- `NOT_TRIAGED`: existing source material not yet evaluated against this dimension.
- `RESEARCHED_INCOMPLETE`: checked but proof missing or only historical/secondary.
- `SOURCE_SUPPORTED`: scoped legally relevant original official source and date recorded in `PROVENANCE.csv`; identify *exact function and territory* in `review_note`. **Does not mean legal opinion or clinical approval.**
- `CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED`: authoritative evidence or control/effective-date conflict persists.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE_WITH_EVIDENCE`: original official legal/jurisdiction evidence supports inapplicability to the defined M49 population/function; **never infer merely from zero permanent inhabitants**.

`legal_research_acceptance`: `NOT_MEASURED` (initial), `PARTIAL`, `RESEARCH_COMPLETE_PENDING_EXPERT_REVIEW`. Research completion may be recorded **only when all applicable dimensions are source-supported or evidence-backed inapplicable, current original text and commencement are inspected, all issuer conflicts reconciled or narrowly scoped, and `verified_legal_source_ids` are existing precise official `PROVENANCE.csv` IDs**. Evaluate disputed/delegated/renamed cases against **two genuinely independent official checks**, distinguishing publishing websites from **independently issuing** laws. Legal research completion remains **not legal approval** and cannot authorize clinical guidance. An inapplicable domain still requires source, reasons and issuer. Never derive any dimension status just from counts.

`review_note` must record what the legal source *actually* proves, original language, legal issuer versus document host, operative/historical/draft state, relevant jurisdiction/people, source conflict and precise remaining legal question. Where such evidence is missing, leave `NOT_TRIAGED` or `RESEARCHED_INCOMPLETE`. Update `last_legal_review_utc` only after a real review. Do not confuse current organizational identity, current enacted competence and practical implementation.

`data/research_queue.csv` `queue_state`: `QUEUED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `EVIDENCE_ADDED_NOT_RESOLVED`, `BLOCKED_NEW_SOURCE_REQUIRED`, `RESEARCH_CLOSED_FOR_NOW`. The final queue state means the scoped research task is closed, **not law signed off**. Source blocks must have `blocked_reason` and changed-retrieval next action; completed work must have a dated `last_outcome`. Rotate provisional areas regularly, including every UN populated region.
