## A49-581-01 — signed original Department of the Interior Secretary's Order 3224 (18 January 2001)

Original 3-page government-hosted PDF, **pages 1–2 visually inspected on 2026-11-06**:
https://www.doi.gov/sites/default/files/elips/documents/3224_-palmyra_atoll_national_wildlife_refuge.pdf

Section 3 transferred the Secretary of the Interior's **civil administrative** executive, legislative and judicial authority over Palmyra to the **U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS)**, **except** the section 4 excluded areas. Those remained within the **Office of Insular Affairs (OIA)** *until the FWS Director accepts jurisdiction in writing*. Section 4 precisely limits the excluded footprints to specified **tidal or submerged lands, not water above them**, within **nine circles** associated with munitions, vessels or other suspected legacy debris. Not nine islands, not nine health departments. Section 5 refuge/management delegation remains subject to these exclusions. Original order describes transfers in 2001; it **does not demonstrate a later signed FWS written acceptance, 2026 incumbent, healthcare mandate or site-specific occupational/environmental health implementation**. Do not treat absence of a recovered acceptance as affirmative proof one never existed.
