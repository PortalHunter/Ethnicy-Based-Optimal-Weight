## New function-specific corroboration

1. **July 2026 statutory renumbering is independently confirmed by the committee itself.** REK's dated 23 June 2026 notice states that, from 1 July, helsepersonelloven former **§29 becomes §29e** and former §29b becomes §29f. It describes the relevant REK power as *dispensasjon fra taushetsplikt* for specified research uses of patient-journal/treatment-register information, not broad health-system supervision or blanket research governance. Own-committee source: https://rekportalen.no/fra-1-juli-2026-endring-av-helsepersonelloven-%C2%A7%C2%A7-29-og-29-b/ [A43-744-01].
2. **Separate official health-regulator interpretation of §29e** from Helsedirektoratet describes a narrow, conditional dispensation from confidentiality for access to otherwise protected health information, with purpose, consent feasibility, technical protection, public-interest and minimum-data safeguards. Its text explicitly dates the renumbering to **1 July 2026** and identifies departmental delegability to REK. The general section is not, by itself, a Jan Mayen/Svalbard jurisdictional extension. https://www.helsedirektoratet.no/rundskriv/helsepersonelloven-med-kommentarer/taushetsplikt-og-opplysningsrett/-29e.oppslag-i-journal-for-undervisningsformal [A43-744-02].
3. **Original amending regulation is indexed, but still NOT directly retrieved:** Lovdata's publicly indexed 15 June 2026 FOR-2026-06-15-1084 VI/VII changes 2015-747 §4 sixth paragraph (Svalbard) and §19 fifth paragraph (Jan Mayen) to §29e / REK Nord, effective **1 July 2026**. Attempt to open the official original LTI page returned **405 Method Not Allowed** this cycle. Treat as **official indexed original with inaccessible full readback**, not independent authentication of the entire current consolidated 2015-747 or proof no later amendment exists: https://lovdata.no/dokument/LTI/forskrift/2026-06-15-1084 [A43-744-05]. The Helsedirektoratet and REK pages are independently issuing institutions corroborating the *function*, not a substitute for the precise whole operative 747 consolidation.
4. **Later, already effective 1 November 2026 research-law change is a distinct dimension:** Norwegian Health Ministry's **25 September 2026** release said pure health-register research would no longer automatically require REK prior approval when the new law took effect 1 November. https://www.regjeringen.no/no/aktuelt/nye-regler-om-helseforskning-og-bruk-av-helseopplysninger-i-forskning/id3173606/ [A43-744-03]. Helsedirektoratet's **30 October 2026** circular specifically describes §9(2) exceptions for research only using registries governed by the Health Registry Act, with application/effect **1 November 2026**, while other medical/health research generally retains REK preapproval; some legal-basis, confidentiality and data-release requirements remain separate. https://www.helsedirektoratet.no/rundskriv/helseforskningsloven/soknad-til-rek-om-forhandsgodkjenning-av-medisinsk-og-helsefaglig-forskning [A43-744-04].

## Do not conflate three powers

- **REK Nord §29e** as locally named decision-maker for confidentiality dispensation in Svalbard/Jan Mayen under the amended 747 is **not** a general public-health ministry, professional regulator, or local clinical-service authority.
- **REK ethics preapproval** under the Health Research Act is legally distinct from an exemption from secrecy / legal basis to use data. The November 2026 exemption for certain pure register studies does not automatically waive disclosure safeguards or prove every Svalbard/Jan Mayen research scenario has identical rules.
- **Territorial extension** of each national research/data rule to Svalbard versus Jan Mayen must be verified through the actually enacted current 747, the underlying Act's geographic clause and further regulations; neither the official general guidance nor REK website proves an all-area blanket transfer.
