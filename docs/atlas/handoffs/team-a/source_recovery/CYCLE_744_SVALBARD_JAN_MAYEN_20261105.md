**Status: UNKNOWN retained at the combined M49-area / entire-mandate level.** There is now **substantial official original-language legal evidence for Svalbard and some Jan Mayen provisions**, but it explicitly differentiates subterritories and functions. ## High-value legal sources

- **A40-744-01 — Norwegian Ministry of Health and Care Services, updated 22 April 2026** (Norwegian Nynorsk, government original explanatory page, directly opened): https://www.regjeringen.no/no/tema/helse-og-omsorg/innsikt/helselovgjevinga-pa-svalbard-og-jan-mayen/id2424480/. Public law provisions apply only where **specifically extended** to Svalbard/Jan Mayen. A joint-ministry regulation adapts the Health Personnel, Health Supervision, Specialist Health Services, Infection Control and other statutes. Health and Care Services Act and Patient and User Rights Act generally do **not** apply on Svalbard under this regulation. Some statutes extend to Jan Mayen, but its health-service conditions differ. This is **government interpretation**, not a free-standing replacement for the enacted regulation.
- **A40-744-02 — Lovdata, Regulation FOR-2015-06-22-747** (Norwegian Bokmål, independently maintained legal publication index and text search, direct navigation to /nav form returned 405 but full searchable statutory text and metadata were available via https://lovdata.no/forskrift/2015-06-22-747 ). Original title **«Forskrift om anvendelse av helselover og -forskrifter for Svalbard og Jan Mayen»**. Metadata lists amendments through 2025-06-01. Visible text: Helse Nord RHF must arrange locally appropriate emergency and other healthcare (§3); UNN Longyearbyen Hospital public-health/doctor and outbreak roles (§9–10); Longyearbyen local council municipality-like authority in its planning area, Sysselmesteren in other parts of Svalbard (§10); distinct Jan Mayen provisions (§18 ff). **Original enacted regulation and section-level allocations require a full current consolidated-text crosscheck; do not claim every clause or amendment authenticated from search excerpts alone.**
- **A40-744-03 — Norwegian Directorate of Health, commentary on Health Personnel Act §2** (Norwegian Bokmål, separate directorate publisher, originally indexed): https://www.helsedirektoratet.no/rundskriv/helsepersonelloven-med-kommentarer/lovens-formal-virkeomrade-og-definisjoner/-2.lovens-virkeomrade. Confirms the 2015 extension regulation, Longyearbyen hospital UNN role and health-supervision remit of Statsforvalteren i Troms og Finnmark. A directorate interpretation of the same legislative framework, **not an independent enacted law**.
- **A40-744-04 — Norwegian Government 2026–27 Svalbard budget proposition** (Norwegian Nynorsk; primary published parliamentary proposal, indexed, **proposed budget not law**): https://www.regjeringen.no/no/dokumenter/prop2.-1-s-20262027/id3175417/?ch=1. Separates Helse Nord/UNN responsibilities and Barentsburg company's healthcare from public administration. Proposals are evidence of planned allocations, not guaranteed enacted appropriations or universal individual entitlements.

## Synthesis and open points

This M49 code combines Svalbard and Jan Mayen. Longyearbyen hospital is a provider, Longyearbyen local council and Sysselmesteren have **geographically separated** public-health tasks, Helse Nord regional health enterprise has service responsibility, and Jan Mayen extension provisions require separate review. **Do not promote any one entity as sole authority for the whole combined M49 744** or imply mainland patient rights uniformly apply. No cross-area M49 parent or clinical advice added.
