## New primary-government readbacks

1. **Presidential Decreto Ley Núm. 02/2026, dated 17 June 2026, as reproduced in full text on the Equatorial Guinea government's institutional press site on June 17.** Article 2(16) expressly lists **“Ministerio de Sanidad e Infraestructuras Sanitarias”**. Article 3 states administrative/legal continuity for updated departments that succeed merged/extinguished departments. The sole transitional clause orders implementing organizational rules **within four months of the instrument's coming into force**, preserving existing administrative units' functions provisionally. Crucially, its final clause conditions commencement on **both promulgation and publication in the country's Boletín Oficial del Estado**. The government website's reproduction was directly read, **but a separate BOE issue and publication date were not authenticated**; do not infer legally verified commencement on June 17 from the press-post timestamp alone. Source [A45-226-01]: https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/decreto_ley_que_establece_las_denominaciones_de_los_departamentos_ministeriales .

2. **Presidential Decreto Núm. 60/2026, dated 23 June 2026, also reproduced by the institutional portal.** The signed-name text appoints a minister of **“Sanidad e Infraestructuras Sanitarias”** and a minister-delegate with the same portfolio. This supports **June identity and appointments**, not clinical powers or independent proof of publication of Decreto Ley 02/2026. Both documents originate in the **Presidency** and are reproduced by **one government press portal**: count two document types, **not two independently issuing legal authorities**. Source [A45-226-02]: https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/decreto_de_nombramiento_del_viceprimer_ministro_y_demas_miembros_del_gobierno .

3. **Vicepresidency/Government report dated 30 October 2026** on the national health emergency committee/malaria programme instead calls the minister's portfolio **“Sanidad, Bienestar Social e Infraestructuras Sanitarias”**. This is a **later contemporaneous nomenclature conflict** with Article 2(16) and the June appointment text. It is a government **communications report, not an amending law**. The malaria programme's coordinating activities do not establish the ministry's full statutory remit, nor ownership of all INSESO hospitals. Source [A45-226-03]: https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/index.php/noticias/el_comite_nacional_de_emergencia_sanitaria_presenta_la_proyeccion_para_el_2030 .

4. **Vicepresidency's 1 July 2026 official report** describes INSESO restructuring and outstanding administrative appointments; it does not independently announce or authenticate **completion** of the previously envisaged six-month hospital/property transfer. The February/March government reports were prospective, and a September target date is not a completion certificate. Source [A45-226-04]: https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_prioriza_la_reestructuracion_de_inseso_ante_nuevas_contrataciones .

## Carefully limited conclusion

**Identity discovery is materially advanced:** an original-language presidential decree-law reproduction and appointment decree both identify the June portfolio, while later government reporting introduces an exact contradictory expanded label. **Do not promote M49 226 to a sole verified present-mandate authority yet.** The June instrument's official-gazette promulgation/publication proof, subsequent amendment or implementing decrees, competing October naming, current organogram, and INSESO hospital asset/personnel transfer acceptance remain unavailable. Gazette verification is distinct from an institutional web article; same publisher and presidency cannot be counted twice as independent enactment issuers. The social-welfare portfolio's allocation between health and social-affairs departments must be checked against actual subsequent regulations.
