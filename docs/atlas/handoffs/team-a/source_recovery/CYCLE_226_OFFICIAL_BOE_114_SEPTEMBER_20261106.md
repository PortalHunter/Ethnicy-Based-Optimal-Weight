## New official-gazette primary document: Decreto Núm. 114/2026, 11 September 2026

The **Equatorial Guinea official Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE)** lists on its [latest-publications page](https://boe.gob.gq/ultimaspublicaciones) a 11 September 2026 item for **“NOMBRAMIENTO DE LOS SECRETARIOS GENERALES DE LOS DEPARTAMENTOS MINISTERIALES”**, with linked four-page scanned [original presidential decree 114/2026](https://boe.gob.gq/files/DECRETOS%20DE%20SECRETARIOS%20GENERALES%20DE%20LOS%20DEPARTAMENTOS%20MINISTERIALES.pdf). The downloaded PDF was inspected visually on **page 1** (title/date/presidential seal) and **page 3** (appointments), rather than relying on an indexed snippet. Its footer carries an electronic verification code. **Item 15, page 3**, expressly uses the original-language ministry title **`Ministerio de Sanidad e Infraestructuras Sanitarias`** and names a Secretary General; **item 18** separately lists **`Ministerio de Igualdad de Género y Asuntos Sociales`**. These are contemporaneous signed **appointment** records. [A46-226-01]

**Evidentiary advance:** an actual original document available from the country's BOE site independently **hosts** the September institutional title, unlike Batch45's June documents reproduced by a government press office. This is *stronger documentary corroboration of nomenclature as of 11 September*, not evidence that health policy is fully assigned, that social welfare is absent from the health portfolio at every date, or that health ministries control all hospitals. The decree's issuing authority is **still the Presidency**, as in the June instruments: BOE hosting is a distinct publication channel, **not a second independent issuing legal authority**.

**Critical non-inference:** This is **Decreto 114/2026**, not the required original **Decreto Ley 02/2026**. Finding a September BOE-hosted scanned appointment **does NOT prove** that the June decree-law was promulgated and published as required by its own final clause. Nor does it refute the **30 October** expanded title `Sanidad, Bienestar Social e Infraestructuras Sanitarias`: a later enacted renaming/reallocation remains possible, and the October communication itself is not legal text. Similarly, this decree makes **no finding** on INSESO hospital property/personnel transfer or patient-care responsibilities. No inferred M49 parent/delegation/sole-provider authority.
