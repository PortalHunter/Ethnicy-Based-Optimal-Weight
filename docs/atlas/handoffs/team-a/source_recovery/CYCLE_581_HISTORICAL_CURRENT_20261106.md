# Team A Batch42 — M49 581 historical/current island administration conflict (2026-11-06 UTC)

## Documented correction: never treat a 1989 DOI manual as current jurisdiction

The directly retrieved US Interior **Departmental Manual 575 DM 3**, section 3.8, is expressly **effective 27 April 1989** (front matter says later re-release has *no text changes*). Its attributions of Midway to the Navy and Navassa to the Coast Guard therefore **do not prove current authority**: FWS's own Midway history places full jurisdiction with FWS from **1996**, and previously read Interior **Secretarial Order 3210 (1999)** expressly transferred Navassa civil administration to FWS, superseding an older order. Historical descriptions of Johnston's Defense Nuclear Agency and Air Force must also not be used as current allocations. The Department Manual is retained as a source of historical change, not an operative 2026 health-law citation. [A42-581-01; A42-581-02; A41-581-03]

FWS currently describes conservation staff, contractors and restricted airfield/refuge activity on **Midway**, but that is not a health-policy delegation. FWS's Johnston page requires both Air Force approval and refuge-purpose suitability for access; the mixed access roles neither establish a single civil jurisdiction nor identify an occupational or public-health officer. Its Pacific Islands Heritage Marine National Monument page describes distinct Baker, Howland, Jarvis, Johnston, Kingman, Palmyra and Wake refuges, and a January 2025 renaming from **Pacific Remote Islands Marine National Monument**. This conservation geography does not substitute for all nine M49 581 component health authorities or include Midway's separately described reserve within the same monument. [A42-581-03; A42-581-04; A42-581-05]

Preserve Batch41's original **32 CFR part 935 (2025 annual)** Wake sanitation/quarantine powers and original Navassa SO3210 / Palmyra SO3224 exceptions. Do **not** infer that the historical 1989 manual repeals later signed orders. Neither the 2025 CFR annual edition nor own-agency refuge web pages independently certify the exact November 2026 eCFR version, redelegations, or military/contractor medical obligations.
