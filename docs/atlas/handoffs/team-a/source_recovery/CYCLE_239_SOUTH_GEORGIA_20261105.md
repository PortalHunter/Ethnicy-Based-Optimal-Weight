# Research Cycle 3/6 — M49 239 South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands (2026-11-05)

**Status: UNKNOWN retained.** Legal authority, visitor medical provision and worker welfare must be checked separately. Earlier B39-239 observations stand.

## Source observations

- **A40-239-01 — GSGSSI legal ordinance index** (English, government-associated legal guide, directly opened): https://laws.gov.gs/ordinances/. The website **expressly denies legal status for itself**, and identifies the **South Georgia and South Sandwich Islands Gazette as the publication of record since 3 October 1985** (the Falkland Islands Gazette before that). An informal index, even of purportedly current ordinances, does not independently authenticate an enacted consolidated ordinance.
- **A40-239-02 — GSGSSI policies list** (English, same governmental publication context; linked policy PDF **not read**): https://gov.gs/documents_policies/. Lists a *Health, Safety and Welfare Policy*. Presence of a policy title is not evidence of a health authority's legal powers or which staff it covers.
- **A40-239-03 — GSGSSI visitor risk policy** (English, same government; source opened/indexed): https://gov.gs/visitor-policy-risk-awareness/. For **visitors**, no medical facilities/search-and-rescue are available and vessels must be medically self-sufficient; its visitor-support condition explicitly distinguishes authorised government researchers. It says nothing definitive about station employees' welfare, external referral, occupational legislation, or public-health regulation.

## Reconciliation and limitations

The legal portal and visitor policy share an issuer and are **not independent statutory checks**. The Gazette and exact current ordinance texts, the Health/Safety/Welfare policy body, and applicable worker/employer provisions remain unread. No inference of Falklands health delegation, absent local healthcare for all people, or unitary authority across the islands. No new body or cross-area relationship.
