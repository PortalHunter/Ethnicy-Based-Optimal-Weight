## 020 Andorra (original Catalan BOPA PDFs visually checked)

**A54-020-01:** Original one-page [Decret 488/2025](https://bopadocuments.blob.core.windows.net/bopa-documents/037157/pdf/GD_2025_12_24_13_28_07.pdf) in *Butlletí Oficial del Principat d'Andorra* issue 157 of **31 December 2025**, from the Govern / Cap de Govern (not the independent document host). The article and repealing clause revoke the 11 November 2020 creation of the *Laboratori Nacional d'Epidemiologia* and 2021 amendment. Final clause: **effective 1 January 2026**. Historical lab record remains for provenance; it is not counted in current country authority links.

**A54-020-02:** Original three-page [Decret 489/2025](https://bopadocuments.blob.core.windows.net/bopa-documents/037157/pdf/GD_2025_12_24_13_29_33.pdf) in the same BOPA issue and issuing Government, page 2 revised Ministry of Health Article 2 effective **1 January 2026**. National Ministry functions: health resources / medicines / authorization of providers, disease prevention/epidemiological surveillance, environmental/food safety, **Laboratori Central de Salut Pública** and strategic planning. The separate National Epidemiology Laboratory is no longer listed. Same governmental issuer as 488, **not** two independent enactment issuers.

The [Ministry structure page](https://www.govern.ad/ca/ministeris-i-secretaries-d-estat/ministeri-de-salut/el-ministeri/estructura-del-ministeri) still lists the formerly founded lab. Primary published decree prevails for this narrow 2025/2026 point; subsequent 2026 amendments, any replacement functions, specialist medicines/professional regulator laws and municipal law not checked. No clinical advice.

## 036 Australia (Commonwealth only)

**A54-036-01:** [Administrative Arrangements Order](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025Q00003/2025-07-01/2025-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html) signed by Governor-General **13 May 2025** on Federal Executive Council advice, compiled with **1 July 2025** amendments in the official Federal Register (in-force version shown). Part 9 allocates federal health protection, disease prevention, human quarantine, medical research and therapeutic-goods regulation to Health, Disability and Ageing; Part 6 separately allocates work health and safety to Employment and Workplace Relations. Not complete statutory powers of agencies or state/territory/Indigenous/municipal authorities, and later 2026 legal changes not audited.
