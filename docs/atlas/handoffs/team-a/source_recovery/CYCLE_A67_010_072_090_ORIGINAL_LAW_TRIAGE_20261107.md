## 090 Solomon Islands — A67-090-01
- **Original government Attorney-General's Chambers authorised reprint** of Health Services Act (Cap 100), English, expressly **as in force 5 February 2024**: https://attorneygenerals.gov.sb/wp-content/uploads/dlm_uploads/2024/09/Health-Services-Act-Cap.-100v3_as-at-050224.pdf . Direct PDF visually inspected pp. 1 (cover), 4 (printed p2, §§3-4) and 12 (endnotes) on 2026-11-07; text also checked for §§10-12.
- Parliament is legal issuer; Attorney-General authorised publication is not an independent second *enacting* body. §3 legally describes Ministry and Permanent Secretary, §4 ministerial primary-care role, §§10-12 allow specific arrangements with Provincial Assemblies and Honiara Town Council and their own clinics. The **existence of a power to arrange** does not prove any actual 2026 local agency delegation or sole national provider. Endnotes show 2023 Act 17, 2024-02-05 effective amendment to §14, and 2009 amendment to §8.
- Narrow establishing, primary-health and framework territorial-devolution dimensions source-supported **as of the official 2024 reprint only**. Amendments after 2024, current ministry organisational instruments, public health surveillance, medicines/pharmacy, health-professions law, local instruments and independent issuing source checks remain open. **No all-tier legal acceptance.**

## 010 Antarctica — A67-010-01
- **Australian Capital Territory official legislative register**, Public Health Act 1997 A1997-69, marked **In Force**, current R45 effective **26 Nov 2025**, administered by ACT health portfolios, with a **proposed** 2026 bill clearly separate: https://legislation.act.gov.au/a/1997-69/ . Read government register HTML 2026-11-07, not an original Territory Antarctic extension/operation order.
- Earlier A55-010-01 Australian Antarctic Territory Act 1954 §6 provides only *qualified, sector-limited* application of appropriate ACT/Jervis Bay laws. Checking the ACT source is not proof the law applies to every Antarctic operator, camp, person or territory. Inspect operative AAT subordinate law, worker-health regulations, medical registration, station implementing rules and foreign national expeditions **individually**. No continent-wide identity; 010 retains UNKNOWN.
