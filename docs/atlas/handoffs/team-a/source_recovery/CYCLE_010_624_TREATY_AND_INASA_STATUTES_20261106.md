## A52-010-01 — treaty's own deposited text, Article VIII (narrow personal jurisdiction)
Source: Antarctic Treaty Secretariat treaty repository, https://documents.ats.aq/recatt/att005_e.pdf ; English 1959 Treaty text; retrieved via machine-readable PDF text on 2026-11-06, **PDF screenshot attempt returned unsupported content type**. Legally issuing parties: original Treaty contracting States; web host: **Antarctic Treaty Secretariat**, custodian/reproducer rather than a health-law issuer. Article IV preserves opposing territorial-claim positions, Article VI sets treaty area south of 60° S and protects high seas rights, Article VII(5) requires notices of expeditions, nationally occupied stations and certain military personnel; Article VIII(1) assigns **nationality jurisdiction only to designated Article VII observers, exchanged Article III scientific personnel and accompanying staff for acts in their treaty functions**. Article VIII(2) sends other jurisdiction disputes to consultations; Article IX(1)(e) provides a jurisdiction-measures forum. **Cannot infer all Antarctic residents/contractors, every station, health policy, disease surveillance, clinical standards or medical/worker-health regulation are under one flag.** Treaty itself is not a 2026 state health act or all-national expedition-by-expedition allocation.
