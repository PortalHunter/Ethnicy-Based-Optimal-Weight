## First-party evidence

- **A40-334-01 — Australian Antarctic Division, territory governance** (English, Australian Government department, original page inspected): https://www.antarctica.gov.au/about-antarctica/australia-in-antarctica/the-territory-of-heard-island-and-mcdonald-islands/. Identifies **Heard Island and McDonald Islands Act 1953** and the hierarchy of laws: Commonwealth Acts explicitly extended to HIMI; Territory ordinances; ACT non-criminal laws insofar as applicable and consistent; Jervis Bay Territory criminal law. States the Australian Antarctic Division administers the Territory for the Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water. **This is territorial/environmental administration, not a finding that the agency has every public-health power.**
- **A40-334-02 — Australian Antarctic Division, territory-legislation list** (English, same issuing division; original page opened): https://www.antarctica.gov.au/antarctic-operations/stations-and-field-locations/heard-island/protection-and-management/legislation/territory-legislation/. Enumerates the 1953 Act, Environmental Protection and Management Ordinance 1987, Criminal Procedures Ordinance 1993 and Weapons Ordinance 2001; summaries/links are **not** a separately reviewed consolidated signed legal text.
- **A40-334-03 — Australian Antarctic Division, permitting** (English, same department; original indexed content reviewed): https://www.antarctica.gov.au/antarctic-operations/stations-and-field-locations/heard-island/protection-and-management/permits/. Visitors must be self-sufficient and are subject to permit and liability requirements. **No medical provider/regulator or worker statutory duty is identified in this source.**

## Legal inference boundary

The HIMI legal regime **expressly contemplates application of certain external Australian laws**, so remoteness and no permanent human occupation do not imply no legal responsibility. But neither an environmental permit nor general Australia-wide Polar Medicine proves a HIMI-specific public-health delegation. Three A40 observations share the **same issuer**, not three independent corroborations.
