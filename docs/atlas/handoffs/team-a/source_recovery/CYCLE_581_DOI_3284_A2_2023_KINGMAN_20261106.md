## A50-581-01 — original signed Kingman Reef Secretary's Order 3223 (18 January 2001)
Original government PDF: https://www.doi.gov/sites/doi.gov/files/elips/documents/3223_-kingman_reef_national_wildlife_refuge.pdf ; sole page **visually inspected 2026-11-06**. Sec. 1 states DOI accepted **restoration from Navy** on **1 September 2000**. Sec. 3 establishes Kingman Reef National Wildlife Refuge and delegates DOI Secretary's **civil administration of the Refuge**, via Assistant Secretary for Fish and Wildlife and Parks, to FWS Director for emerged reef plus submerged lands/waters to **12 nautical miles**, but expressly makes refuge management *consistent with Navy administrative control under 1934/1941 Executive Orders until/unless that control is extinguished*. Sec. 4 says effective immediately. This is **signed original 2001** legal text, not an independently authenticated 2026 reconciliation of the intertwined Navy/DOI functions or health mandates. It does **not** support a blanket claim that Navy control fully ended in 2000.
