## 044 — Bahamas: public-health law, wellness and dental commencement
- **A65-044-01:** Government law revision English Chapter 231 *Health Services Act*, original 1914 statute, revised pages LRO 1/2017. Government PDF page labelled CH231-7 (visual read, PDF index 6), s3: established Health Department headed by Chief Medical Officer, principal adviser/CEO to Health Minister. Sections 8-11 infectious-disease notifications and response. **This is narrow historical/delegated health-law evidence, not proof 2026 amended all-tier powers.**
- **A65-044-02:** Parliament Act 69/2025, signed/assented and published in official extraordinary Gazette 28 Aug 2025. Original Gazette cover and printed pp3/7 visually read. Sections 4/8/9 place narrow national health promotion, nutrition policy and cross-ministerial functions with responsible Minister; s13 national Food and Nutrition Commission; s18 expressly preserves other food-safety/standards powers. Do not generalize this wellness act to all infectious disease, health licensure or medication controls. Check subsequent 2026 amendments and actual implementing regulations.
- **A65-044-03:** Parliament Act 71/2025 *Dental Professions Act*, original Gazette printed pp3/6 visually read. Section 1(2) **requires separate ministerial Appointed Day Notice**; government laws index lists a 2026 notice but its original notice and actual commencement text were not retrieved. Sections 3-4 describe proposed continuation/registration remit but are **not promoted as 2026 operative** without exact notice. Same legislature issuer as Act69; hosting by same law archive is not independent statutory issuer.

## 050 — Bangladesh: original Bangla 2010 professional-regulator law vs draft ordinance
- **A65-050-01:** Ministry of Law government statute database directly provides Bangla 2010 Act 61 `বাংলাদেশ মেডিকেল ও ডেন্টাল কাউন্সিল আইন, ২০১০`. Section 1(2) says immediately effective; s3 constitutes Bangladesh Medical and Dental Council; s5 enumerates medical/dental training recognition, registration, discipline/ethics; ss18-23 practitioner registers and practice controls; s38 repeals 1980 law. This supports only a 2010 **professional council statute**, not MOHFW establishing statute or all medicines law. Through-2026 repeal/amendments unreviewed.
- **A65-050-02:** MOHFW Medical Education and Family Welfare Division's government laws page lists the 2010 BMDC Act and separately a **draft** `বাংলাদেশ মেডিকেল এন্ড ডেন্টাল কাউন্সিল অধ্যাদেশ, ২০২৫ (খসড়া)`. This is no proof a replacement ordinance entered force; seek actual independently issued 2025/2026 Gazette before revision. The ministry's divisions, district powers, DGDA medicines, BMRC institute and local statutes are still unscreened.
