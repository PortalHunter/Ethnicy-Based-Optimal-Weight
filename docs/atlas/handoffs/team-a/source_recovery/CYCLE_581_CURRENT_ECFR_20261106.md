## Recovered government-hosted regulatory provisions and limits

1. **Wake substantive health/public-safety powers — §935.12, eCFR text displayed current through 4 November 2026.** The official Office of the Federal Register eCFR section directly enumerates the Commander's powers to direct sanitation/fire inspections, establish vital statistics, impose quarantines, impound unsanitary food, evacuate people from hazardous areas and issue health/safety protective orders. It does **not** make the Commander the sole medical provider, clinical regulator or health ministry for M49 581. The site expressly says continuously updated eCFR is **authoritative but unofficial**, not an official legal edition of the CFR. [A44-581-01] https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/subtitle-A/chapter-VII/subchapter-N/part-935/subpart-B/section-935.12

2. **Civil delegation — §935.10, eCFR version displayed current through 11 September 2026.** The Secretary of the Air Force holds Wake civil-administration authority, delegated to Air Force General Counsel with tightly specified written redelegation provisions; Commander acts as designee only for functions assigned under this part. The direct section was read, but actual assignment/redelegation writings, current occupant and day-to-day worker/provider arrangements have **not** been authenticated. This is not a country-wide or nine-island delegation. [A44-581-02] https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/subtitle-A/chapter-VII/subchapter-N/part-935/subpart-B/section-935.10

3. **Geographic limitation — §935.1, eCFR version displayed current through 14 August 2026.** This code names **Wake, Peale and Wilkes** plus associated reefs/waters/airspace, rather than all component islands aggregated under UN M49 581. It leaves the other eight named M49 outlying-island components and separate legal instruments to be reviewed. The differently dated live page displays should not be silently conflated into a single same-day legal-completeness certification. [A44-581-03] https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/subtitle-A/chapter-VII/subchapter-N/part-935/subpart-A/section-935.1

4. **Authentic original amendment source — 90 FR 34763 (24 July 2025), FR Doc 2025-13994.** The Air Force final amendment changes **only §935.3(d)** (terminology in a definition) and explicitly calls that edit administrative. It did **not** amend the health powers in §935.12 or enlarge M49 geography; however, it cannot establish *absence* of any other amendments or later redelegations through November 2026.
