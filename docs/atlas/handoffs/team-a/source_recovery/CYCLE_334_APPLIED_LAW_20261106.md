## Applied-law mechanism now directly read

The Federal Register lists **C2008C00326** as the latest in-force compilation of the 1953 Act. Following its document link recovered the complete legislative text after guessed direct endpoints failed.

Sections **5–8** distinguish ACT non-criminal law, Jervis Bay criminal law, expressly extending Commonwealth Acts and local ordinances. Section **6(1)** normally carries the relevant applied-law function-holder into the territory; **6(2)** permits a Governor-General direction substituting another person or authority. This is a legal route to identifying responsibility, not proof that AAD or the ACT Health Directorate holds every function. Exceptions, applicable health enactments and any directions must be checked first.

The Register's territorial guide independently explains administration by AAD but is the same publishing institution, not a second legal issuer. Environmental administration cannot substitute for an enacted public-health allocation. This cycle records the mechanism and a testable next step without assigning a parent M49 or inventing a local ministry.
