## What success means — and does not mean

The original assignment is an **independently source-traceable census of nongovernmental professional societies, condition organizations, independent biomedical/academic groups and community-controlled/subnational bodies** for the full UN M49 geography and seven conditions `diabetes`, `hypertension`, `high_chol`, `gout`, `lactose`, `celiac`, `hemochromatosis`. Deliver verified identities and exact original-language resources where available; distinguish source/page publisher, original document authors/issuer, organization domicile, surveyed population and healthcare/legal jurisdiction. Maintain 10+ anti-overgeneralization cases and explicit Indigenous governance and cross-border caveats. No inference from national residency/ethnicity/ancestry to individual health status.

All **248×7 = 1,736** area-condition cells already exist structurally: this is **not** 100% substantive coverage. A cell may correctly remain **bounded searched/unlinked** after documented attempts; do not invent a society, infer national absence, force a foreign society into local domicile, or fabricate a "positive" resource to reach 100%. Likewise, a linked educational source is **not** a clinically accepted guideline. Keep all `clinical_release_state=research_lead_only`; clinical appraisal belongs to [issue #27](https://github.com/PortalHunter/Ethnicy-Based-Optimal-Weight/issues/27). Team A covers government authorities, C primary journal census, D WHO/international networks. Do not duplicate their ownership.

Measure three different stages separately: (1) **identity reach** = M49 areas with a verified locally domiciled organization / 248, (2) **source-positive reach** = distinct cells with ≥1 eligible linked source / 1,736, and (3) **search disposition** = positive + bounded-unlinked + untouched (total 1,736). A source-positive link is a research lead, not clinical, legal, completeness or license approval. Never describe a single scalar as "overall research completion" or claim unattainable geographic coverage is achieved.

## Acceptance gates for each change

- **Organization:** first-party evidence of name, actual locally domiciled seat, clinical/condition relevance, independence/role, current-versus-historical lifecycle, exact source link, language, checked date and provenance. If it is cross-border or only foreign-based, use documented service scope, **not** a false new area identity.
- **Exact condition publication:** a real URL for that specific condition and the linked organization's actual `authored`, `jointly_authored`, `endorsed`, `hosted`, `section_published`, `registry`, or `patient_education` role. Check original-language title, publication/edition year, named authors, edition conflicts and relationship; third-party index alone is not proof of society original authorship.
- **Context/provenance:** update `condition_organizations.csv`, `publication_context.csv`, `PROVENANCE.csv`, `search_log.csv`, and the **exact** `coverage_by_area_condition.csv` cell in sync. Keep `source_country_basis` within the validator's allowed values `issuer_of_linked_page`, `document_issuer`, `journal_publication_country`, or `unresolved`; use original imprint rather than host geography where verified. Record organization domicile, source country and basis, participant group/denominator, healthcare jurisdiction and evidentiary caveats. Cross-area original authorship needs cross-area provenance. All contexts remain `research_lead_only`.
- **Disease/ethics guardrails:** FH ≠ elevated LDL/statin use; clinical gout ≠ elevated urate; hereditary haemochromatosis ≠ ferritin; lactase genotype ≠ symptomatic lactose intolerance; celiac HLA/seropositivity ≠ confirmed diagnosis. Do not generalize national samples to all ancestries or presume Indigenous permission/control. Preserve documented conflicting numbers or historical/current versions instead of silently settling them.
- **Unsuccessful check:** log real source, local language query/navigation, date, retrieval result, negative scope and limits; only mark `targeted_search_no_verified_resource` with actual search logs. Do **not** assert no national body or source exists. For hard low-population M49 cases, note no apparent eligible local specialist identity as **evidence-limited, still unresolved**. Do not create fictitious sources or mark verification achieved because a page is an index.
- **No destructive deduplication:** preserve earlier provenance and historical search records; add corrections with clear provenance rather than erasing former conflicts. Check exact organization/condition/URL uniqueness and joins.

## Batch selection, failure avoidance and stopping rules

Work in focused batches with **concrete exit outcomes**: source-positive cell gained, genuinely independently verified local identity gained, issuer imprint resolved with original evidence, named conflict corrected with traceable source, or bounded search documented with explicit reason for no eligible source. A newly created provenance row alone is not success. **Quality corrections and independently documented search-depth upgrades count as meaningful progress; superficial index-only source links do not.** Record a concrete per-task outcome in `QUEUE.md`, including next escalation question; rotate geography and condition after repeated nonfindings. Do not prioritize inaccessible/uninhabited territories solely to improve the 227/248 bar; track them honestly. Source-count and organization-count increases without new geographic reach are legitimate but not substitutes for cell coverage.

Prefer a flexible mix of **6–15 different lower-risk first-pass cells and 2–5 deeper source, issuer, search-depth or quality audits** when source access permits. Smaller, rigorous runs are appropriate; these are not quotas for manufactured findings, boilerplate searches or invented organizations. Every five substantive data batches, schedule a focused high-risk review of earlier source-positive links. If blocked on source licensing, legal incorporation, inaccessible originals, community consent or metadata conflicts, carry forward as evidence-limited with a named next verification step or escalate for independent human review. Do not run indefinite same-query loops.

Do not declare original acceptance complete unless the full 248×7 matrix, source identity/country/population/jurisdiction fields, local original-language verification states and 10+ diversity cases have been audited and remaining nonpositive cells and issuer imprints are explicitly classified, with unverified work **still clearly flagged**. Legal rights, actual clinical adoption and medical claim appraisal are **separate approvals**, not something Team B may certify. It is acceptable to report **research substantially inventoried but verification unresolved**.
