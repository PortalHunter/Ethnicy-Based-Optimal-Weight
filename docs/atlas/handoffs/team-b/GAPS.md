# Team B gaps and open questions

- The 248 × 7 M49 coverage matrix is a **discovery-status skeleton**, not completed organization research. All unreviewed cells are explicitly `not_yet_researched`; never infer that a condition-specific body does not exist.
- Direct machine-readable comparison of the 248-code roster against current UNSD M49 overview remains to be independently repeated. The roster excludes ISO numeric 158 (Taiwan), absent from the UNSD overview. UN M49 statistical practice is not a sovereignty statement.
- Country/area authorities in territories, areas without a permanent population, and cross-border societies require nuanced remit investigation. No fabricated society assigned.
- Individual source relationships, governance structure, publication status, copyright and guideline currency require a second evidence review. The Diabetes Canada source currently resolves under `dev.diabetes.ca`; locate a canonical official destination.
- France-based Société Francophone du Diabète is **not** automatically the guideline authority for all Francophone states. Coeliac UK reports BSG's 2026 guidelines; it is **not** recorded as the BSG guideline author. Collaboration does not imply endorsement.
- Indigenous health data require permissions and community governance. FNIGC's OCAP® principles are not a license to reproduce raw survey data. Lowitja and Te Atawhai each have distinct governance and remit.
- No clinical propositions have been promoted into the product. #27 controls claim appraisal.

## Broad-label conflation failure cases (review examples, not genetic assertions)

| Case | Unsafe flattening | Required distinction before any health inference |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Canada | “Canadian” = one ancestry | First Nations / Inuit / Métis / settlers / immigration and particular Nations; FNIGC on-reserve data scope |
| 2. Australia | “Australian” = one ancestry | Distinct Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples; community controlled organizations and non-Indigenous residents |
| 3. Aotearoa New Zealand | “Māori” = a single iwi or dataset | Whanganui iwi, other iwi/hapū and differing research governance |
| 4. Brazil | “Brazilian” = shared inherited phenotype | Self-described ethnicities, mixed ancestry, Indigenous nations, regional cohorts |
| 5. Mexico | “Mexican” = uniform biology | Distinct Indigenous peoples, migration, urban/rural cohorts, regional variation |
| 6. India | “South Asian” = uniform clinical population | State, language, ancestry, religion and specific study cohorts; do not extrapolate a Delhi sample to India |
| 7. Nigeria | “West African” = one clinical phenotype | Distinct languages, ethnic communities and contexts within and beyond Nigeria |
| 8. Ethiopia | “East African” = a single genetic profile | Distinct local language groups and communities; highland/lowland sampling differences |
| 9. South Africa | “African” = homogeneous | Multiple self-identification systems, subnational populations, social determinants and cohorts |
| 10. China | “Chinese” = one research cohort | National, provincial, linguistic and demographic differences; separately coded Hong Kong and Macao areas |
| 11. Indonesia | “Southeast Asian” = all residents alike | Different islands, peoples, language groups and migration patterns; individual study settings |
| 12. Philippines | “Filipino” = single phenotype | Distinct ethnolinguistic and Indigenous communities and study subgroups |
| 13. Papua New Guinea | “Melanesian” = identical to a particular community | Village/linguistic diversity and research-specific community permissions |
| 14. United Kingdom | “British” = all ethnic subgroups or all UK healthcare systems | England/Scotland/Wales/Northern Ireland healthcare responsibilities, diaspora and separate genetic ancestry |

These examples flag *metadata and study-transportability errors* to check; they do not claim differential rates or recommend individualized actions.


## New findings and review priorities after region-spanning expansion (2026-11-04)

- **Coverage:** 33 of 248 M49 areas have any specialist organization record. Only 57 of 1,736 area-condition pairs have a linked source; **1,679 have not yet been researched**. National/regional organization presence does not imply population or diaspora relevance.
- **Discontinued:** The [Nederlandse Diabetes Federatie](https://diabetesfederatie.nl/) states that its activities ended **January 1, 2026**, and that resources were distributed to a number of other bodies. Former NDF products **must not** be displayed as current-NDF guidelines or assigned to a new owner without evidence.
- **Joint authoring / hosting:** The [Philippine DOH guideline portal](https://doh-gabay.telehealth.ph/view_guideline.php?id=85) credits **PCEDM** as lead developer and multiple partners; DOH hosts/approves. The [Thai endocrine society](https://www.thaiendocrine.org/guideline/) hosts a 2023 guideline with multiple issuing groups. [Italian SID/AMD](https://aemmedi.it/linee-guida-e-raccomandazioni/) co-author a DM2 document; this is one underlying evidence object, with multiple organization edges rather than independent replications.
- **Moroccan website provenance:** [SMEDIAN](https://smedian.net/) has a society scientific-congress page; original society membership and exact guideline publication need independent appraisal. Another Moroccan-looking SMD website ([smdiabete.org](https://smdiabete.org/)) includes text saying Société **Malienne** de Diabétologie, creating an unresolved identity/website-copy inconsistency; **not counted** as an established separate Moroccan guideline issuer.
- **Patient/funder versus guideline author:** Norwegian Diabetesforbundet supports external research, Diabetes NZ co-commissioned a PwC report, Diabetes Singapore links to government-authored resources, and Canadian Hemochromatosis Society lists provincial and international guidelines. None of these facts alone verifies scientific authorship or endorses the third-party guidance.
- **Data and copyright:** Japan's [hypertension society](https://www.jpnsh.jp/guideline.html) explicitly restricts the reproduction of its 2025 guideline. Maintain URL and bibliographic index only. Do not copy PDF/full-text bodies into the repo.
- **Study population:** Finnish Diabetesbarometri 2025 is a survey, not a whole-population genetic or clinical phenotype estimate. AFDiAG Vie100Glut' uses testimony and local survey scope. Do not treat national survey participants as ancestry-linked risk cohorts.
- **Disputed or oversimplified medical statements:** Association education pages can lag evolving clinical recommendations, including some celiac diagnostic criteria, older gout patient leaflets and South African diabetes guidance. Record the linked source as a lead, not a clinical recommendation. Explicit current clinical appraisal required under #27.
- **Condition scarcity:** Outside the US, direct lactose-specific professional sources are especially scarce in this batch; celiac, inherited FH, gout and iron-overload still have large regional gaps. Distinguish actual searches with no body found from no search attempted.
