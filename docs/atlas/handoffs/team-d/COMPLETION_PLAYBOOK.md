## Case-level work protocol and stop rules

For every selected case, preserve an auditable update in its existing Team D dataset/queue or Team D-owned gap log, with at least:
- **Stable record key and category** (WHO M49, April office evidence, EMA regulator ID, named network conflict), prior state and new state.
- **Exact proposition needed for closure** (e.g. “current individual WHO administrative assignment confirmed by WHO primary authority”, not “appears in a PAHO dashboard”).
- **Primary URLs, document title/publisher, publication/point-in-time date, claimed effective date, access mode** (direct page/PDF inspected, official indexed snippet only, archival, HTTP 403/other failure) and section/page where possible.
- **Outcome and reason**: `DIRECTLY_VERIFIED`, `DOCUMENTED_BOUNDARY_OR_NOT_APPLICABLE`, `CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED`, `PRIMARY_SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`, `PENDING_EXACT_IDENTITY_OR_INSTRUMENT`, or `NOT_YET_REVIEWED`. Use these as *research-workflow labels* unless the existing table schema has equivalent controlled vocabulary; don't silently repurpose dataset status fields.
- **What was attempted and what would change the result**: authoritative source/contact/instrument required, and the next viable retrieval route or explicit external legal/WHO/maintainer escalation.

**Source saturation:** After two genuinely distinct authoritative retrieval routes for a narrowly defined blocker fail or repeat the same inadequate evidence, record their URLs/errors/timestamps and move to another case for that run. This is a *per-run switching rule*, not proof the fact is false or permanently unresolvable. Revisit only with a new primary lead, changed source access, relevant stakeholder reply, or explicit source-update date.

**Closure threshold:** Only increase a *verified* numerator when evidence actually satisfies its original named criterion and required date/identity. A documented “not applicable” or exhausted search is valuable research classification but **does not become a verified WHO assignment, April host or legal audit**. Contradictions stay visible. If the criterion is intrinsically inapplicable to an M49 area, report it as a separate documented boundary rather than deleting the area or changing a denominator silently. Do not invent current assignments, grants, signed instruments or cross-team IDs.

Do not say “Team D complete” simply because the 10-edge or 7-condition counts are met.
