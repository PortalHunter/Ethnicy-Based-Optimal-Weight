How to interpret the evidence

These references come from studies, professional guidance and medical information resources. They do not establish a clinically validated personal ideal weight. Where research concerns populations or genetic variants, it cannot predict a particular person's health or medication response. Some calculator methods are experimental and are explained as such alongside the results.

Some links lead to full research papers; others lead to abstracts, professional guidance or secondary summaries. Sources and guidelines may change over time.

Help improve this research

Are you a researcher, clinician, student or interested reader? We welcome new studies, published corrections, contradictory findings and proposals for additional educational modules. You can submit evidence or feedback, or read the scientific contribution guidelines. Please share citations rather than copyrighted full papers, and never post private patient or genomic data.
