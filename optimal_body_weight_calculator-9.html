Ancestry-Adjusted Optimal Body Weight & Food Calculator

Calculates an optimal weight range based on WHO guidance for BMI categories and ethnicity-specific disease-risk thresholds (public-health action points vary by population), wrist/elbow frame size, and published ancestry-linked dietary adaptations. Educational only. [1] [3] [17]







Measurement guidance: Wrist — measure the narrowest circumference just distal to the styloid process (right wrist preferred). MedlinePlus frame size chart. Elbow breadth — with arm flexed at 90°, measure the distance between the medial and lateral epicondyles with a sliding caliper (optional confirmatory measure).

Disclaimer: This is an educational tool based on published WHO guidance, anthropometric methods, and population genetic/dietary research. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. BMI has limitations (does not distinguish muscle vs fat, age, individual variation). Frame size estimates are approximate. Ancestry-based recommendations are population-level patterns, not individual destiny—personal genetics, lifestyle, and clinical status dominate. Consult a physician or registered dietitian before changing diet or weight goals, especially with medical conditions. Sources include WHO Expert Consultation (Lancet 2004) on Asian BMI, NHANES-derived frame methods, and studies on LCT/AMY1/FADS/ALDH2 adaptations.
